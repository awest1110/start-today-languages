Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Start Today Languages ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our content automation application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Data Type What It Is Why We Collect It
OAuth Tokens Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms To authenticate and publish content on your behalf
Account Identifiers Platform-assigned user IDs (not passwords) To route content to the correct accounts
Publishing Logs Timestamps and status of posted content For error handling and confirmation of delivery

We do not collect passwords, payment information, private messages, followers/following lists, or analytics data from your social media accounts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To publish authorized video content to your connected accounts
  • To refresh OAuth tokens before they expire so publishing continues uninterrupted
  • To log delivery status for troubleshooting

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. TikTok API Data

When you connect your TikTok account, we access only the scopes you explicitly authorize (video.upload and video.publish). We comply with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Platform Policy. We do not use TikTok user data to train machine learning models or for any purpose beyond content publishing.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your machine (not on external servers) and are used solely to authenticate API requests. We do not transmit your tokens to any third-party service. You can revoke the App's access at any time through your TikTok account settings under "Manage App Permissions."

5. Data Retention

Tokens and logs are retained only as long as you use the App. You may delete all stored data at any time by revoking app permissions on the connected platforms and removing the App's local configuration files.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You may request a summary of what data the App holds about you.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Revocation: You may revoke platform access at any time through the platform's own settings — no permission from us required.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
